# capitals-as-letters — facts for release notes

**What changed:** on the Commodore-style machines whose letter keys are the
capitals (VIC-20, PET 2001, C128, Plus/4, C64 BASIC and the other
unshifted-case stations), a capital typed or pasted without a physical Shift now
arrives as the plain letter. A visitor who typed `asdfghASDFGH` with Caps Lock
or a paste tool used to get `ASDFGH` and then graphics glyphs; now all twelve
are letters.

**Still reachable:** hold Shift and press a letter and you get the graphics
glyph, as on the real keyboard. The business PETs (CBM 8032, CBM-II), GEOS and
every PC and Unix machine are unchanged.
