## From the summary prose

- (New stations) <u>[Apple //e](station:apple2e) joins as a second Apple II station, next to the 1988 GEOS desktop the museum already showed.</u> Almost nobody actually ran GEOS on an Apple II in the day, so this one boots the machine as it was really used: **ProDOS 8** brings up a one-keypress menu, the Apple II's own version of an `AUTOEXEC.BAT`, offering **AppleWorks**, the 80-column word processor, database and spreadsheet that made the //e a business machine, and **Dazzle Draw**, a double-hi-res paint program that pushed 16 colours out of hardware built for six.

## Bullets

- [Apple //e](station:apple2e)'s STARTUP menu is a tokenized Applesoft program on the boot volume — the exact mechanism ProDOS used for an autoexec in 1985
- Reset always returns [Apple //e](station:apple2e) to its menu: a MAME savestate captured there restores in half a second against a 2.6 second cold boot
- An Apple II Mouse Card is wired into [Apple //e](station:apple2e) for Dazzle Draw, but its cursor does not yet track — the station ships keyboard-only until that is solved
