## A later Spectravideo

The SVI-738 X'Press brought a 3½-inch floppy drive, serial interface and
80-column text into one compact Spectravideo. It remained an MSX1 computer,
even though its V9938 video chip also appeared in MSX2 machines.

## Try it

This station starts at CP/M's `A>` prompt. Type `DIR` to see the working disk,
or `TYPE WELCOME.TXT` for a short introduction. `STAT CON:=UC1:` selects
80-column text; `STAT CON:=CRT:` returns to 40 columns.

The Type demo button starts Microsoft BASIC-80 and types a small program.
Disk loading takes a moment. Press Enter after the final `RUN` to see its
output. In BASIC, `RUN "HELLO"` loads another copy of the example;
`SYSTEM` returns to CP/M. Reset restores the original working disk.

## What is preserved

The emulated machine is the Finland/Sweden model. Its CP/M disk uses the
International keyboard and identifies itself as Spectravideo CP/M-80 2.28,
X'Press release 2.1. The command line, editor and assembler show a different
side of a familiar home computer. BASIC here is a program loaded from disk.
